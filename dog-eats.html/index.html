Chicken and Rice Dog Meal

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup boneless,skinless chicken breast(boiled or baked,no seasoning)
  • 1/2 cooked white rice(plain)
  • cup carrots or green beans(steamed,no salt or butter)
  • 1 tsp olive oil or fish oil(optional,for coat health)

Instruction:

  1. Cook chicken thoroughly(boil or bake,no spices,garlic,or onions).
  2. Chop into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Mixed with cooked rice and vegetables.
  4. Add oil if using,stir well.
  5. Let cool before serving
    6. .

Avoid giving dogs: onions,garlic,grapes,chocolate,avocado, and anythingseasoned or spicy.
